A little help from Bip
1. Create
Write a question, four different choices, the correct answer and an explanation. The standard must be supported by the checker.
2. Check & review
Run checks, then read it as a child would. Compare any scan with the original. Automated checks cannot prove all wording or diagrams correct.
3. Save or approve
Save keeps a private draft. Approve requires your maths, age suitability and privacy review. Neither publishes the question.
4. Release
Download approved content or create a GitHub review request. This does not merge or deploy automatically.
Who can change my content?
This is a single-owner Studio, with no public registration. Initial account creation needs your private server setup token; it stops working once the owner account exists. Future sign-ins use your username and password. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, not readable passwords.
Sessions expire after 30 minutes. Sign out on shared devices. Tailscale remains required. Server administrators can access the underlying files. Passkeys and separate MFA are not connected yet.
Forgot your password?
Sign out, choose Forgot password, and enter one saved recovery code plus a new password. All previous sessions and recovery codes are replaced. Without a recovery code, contact the server administrator; there is no email reset service.
Change your password
You will be signed out and receive new recovery codes. Keep them privately.
What stays private?
Source scans and draft history stay on your server. Family homework is never imported automatically. Approved exports contain question fields only; you must remove names and private information before approval.
Scan not working?
Use a clear printed PNG or JPG, up to 8 MB. OCR may misread symbols and cannot reliably understand handwriting or diagrams. The draft helper extracts one supported Grade 3 arithmetic expression; you can always type your own question.
