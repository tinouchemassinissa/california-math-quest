    Who can change my content?
    This is a single-owner Studio, with no public registration. Initial account creation needs your private server setup token; it stops working once the owner account exists. Future sign-ins use your username and password. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, not readable passwords.
    Sessions expire after 30 minutes. Sign out on shared devices. Tailscale remains required. Server administrators can access the underlying files. Authenticator MFA is available in the Security tab. Passkeys are not connected yet.
    Forgot your password?
    Sign out, choose Forgot password, and enter one saved recovery code plus a new password. All previous sessions and recovery codes are replaced. Recovery also turns off MFA so you can enrol a replacement authenticator. Store recovery codes as carefully as your password. Without a recovery code, contact the server administrator; there is no email reset service.
    Change your password
    You will be signed out and receive new recovery codes. Keep them privately.
    What stays private?
    Source scans and draft history stay on your server. Family homework is never imported automatically. Approved exports contain question fields only; you must remove names and private information before approval.
    Scan not working?
    Use a clear printed PNG or JPG, up to 8 MB. OCR may misread symbols and cannot reliably understand handwriting or diagrams. The draft helper extracts one supported Grade 3 arithmetic expression; you can always type your own question.
    Protect your Studio
    Owner account + your phone’s authenticator. No SMS charges or external QR service.
    Checking protection…
    1 · Add Bip Studio to your authenticator
    In Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Aegis or another TOTP app, choose Add account → Scan QR code. This QR belongs only to your owner account. Never share it.
    On the same phone? Enter a setup key instead

    2 · Confirm the six-digit code
    Setup expires after 10 minutes. MFA remains off until verification succeeds. You’ll receive replacement recovery codes after activation.
    Turn off MFA
    Use a fresh authenticator code. This signs out all sessions and replaces recovery codes.
    Lost your phone?
    Sign out and choose Forgot password. Use one recovery code and set a new password. This revokes sessions and resets MFA; then enrol your replacement phone. Your old password and recovery codes stop working.
    How protection works
    Authenticator codes work offline, expire every 30 seconds, and cannot be reused after acceptance. Failed attempts are rate-limited. Keep your phone’s date and time automatic. Tailscale still protects network access.
    The server must retain your authenticator secret to verify codes. It is stored in the private server database, protected by filesystem permissions—not a separate encrypted vault. Server administrators can access it. Backups must remain private.
